Delete Your Account

We're sorry to see you go. Here's everything you need to know about deleting your CareerOS AI account.

⚠ This action is permanent and cannot be undone.

All of your data will be permanently deleted, including:

  • Uploaded resumes and parsed content
  • Job descriptions and match results
  • AI Coach conversations
  • Mock interview sessions and answers
  • Resume rewrites and roasts
  • Missions, XP, streaks, and achievements
  • Account information and profile

How to Delete Your Account

  1. Open CareerOS AI on your device
  2. Go to the Profile tab (bottom navigation bar)
  3. Scroll down and tap "Delete my account"
  4. Confirm the deletion in the dialog
  5. Your account and all data will be permanently deleted

What Happens After Deletion

  • Immediately: Your account is deactivated and you are signed out
  • Within 30 days: All data is permanently erased from our servers
  • Backup data: Any residual data in backup systems is purged within 90 days

Guest Users

If you are using CareerOS AI as a guest (without an account), your data is stored only on your device. To clear it:

  1. Go to your device's Settings
  2. Find Apps → CareerOS AI
  3. Tap Clear data or Clear storage

Cancel Subscription First

If you have an active Pro subscription, we recommend cancelling it before deleting your account to avoid being charged for the next billing period:

  1. Open the Google Play Store
  2. Tap your profile icon → Payments & subscriptions
  3. Find CareerOS AI and tap Cancel subscription

Need Help?

If you have trouble deleting your account or want to discuss your experience, contact us at rejiranair@gmail.com. We'll help you within 48 hours.
