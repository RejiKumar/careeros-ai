Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 20, 2026  |  Last updated: August 20, 2026

CareerOS AI ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide

  • Account data: email address, display name (when you create an account)
  • Resume content: text extracted from uploaded resume files (PDF, DOCX, TXT)
  • Job descriptions: text you paste for job matching
  • Coach conversations: messages you send to the AI Career Coach
  • Interview responses: answers you provide during mock interviews
  • Feedback: ratings and comments you submit about AI outputs

Information collected automatically

  • Usage data: features used, mission completions, XP earned, app sessions
  • Device information: device model, OS version, app version (for crash reporting)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide and improve the CareerOS AI service
  • Generate AI-powered career guidance (resume scoring, job matching, coaching, interview practice)
  • Track your career progress (XP, levels, streaks, achievements)
  • Send service-related communications (account verification, password resets)
  • Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and security issues
  • Analyze app usage to improve features and performance

3. AI Processing

Your resume content, job descriptions, and conversation messages are sent to third-party AI providers (Google Gemini) for processing. These providers process data on our behalf under strict data protection agreements. AI-generated output is provided for informational purposes only and should be reviewed by you before use.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with:

  • Infrastructure providers: Supabase (database, authentication), Google Cloud (AI processing)
  • Analytics: Firebase Analytics (anonymous usage statistics)
  • As required by law: when compelled by legal process or to protect our rights

5. Data Storage & Security

  • Data is stored in encrypted databases hosted on AWS (ap-northeast-1 region)
  • All data transmission uses TLS encryption
  • Row-level security ensures you can only access your own data
  • We do not store raw resume files after text extraction

6. Data Retention

  • Account data: retained while your account is active
  • Resume and career data: retained until you delete your account or specific items
  • Guest session data: stored locally on your device, not on our servers
  • Upon account deletion, all personal data is permanently removed within 30 days

7. Your Rights

  • Access: view all your data within the app (Profile screen)
  • Export: download your resume and career data
  • Delete: delete your account and all data from the Profile screen
  • Correct: update your information at any time

8. Children's Privacy

CareerOS AI is not intended for users under 16 years of age. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes through the app or via email. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
