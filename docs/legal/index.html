CareerOS AI

Your AI career companion for Android.

CareerOS AI helps you understand your resume, see how it fits a job, practise interviews and plan your next move. AI suggestions are always shown for you to review before they become part of your resume, and scores are estimates, not official ATS results.

What you can do

Resume health score

Import a PDF, DOCX or TXT resume and get a score with the reasons behind it.

Job match & skills gap

Compare your resume with a job description and see matched and missing skills.

Tailor & rewrite

Review suggested wording changes that never add facts you didn't provide.

Interview practice & career coach

Practise answers with guided feedback and ask career questions in chat.

Live job search

Search current openings and track your applications in one place.
