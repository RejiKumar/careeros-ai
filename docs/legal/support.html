Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reset my password?

  1. Open CareerOS AI and go to the Profile tab
  2. Tap "Send password reset link"
  3. Check your email for the reset link
  4. Follow the link to create a new password

How do I upload my resume?

  1. Go to the Resume tab
  2. Tap the upload button and select a PDF, DOCX, or TXT file
  3. Your resume will be analyzed and scored automatically

How does the AI Career Coach work?

The AI Coach provides career guidance based on your uploaded resume and conversation history. Go to the Coach tab to start a conversation. You can ask about career paths, skill gaps, interview prep, and more.

What is the difference between Free and Pro?

Free Tier

Limited daily AI requests across resume scoring, coaching, interview practice, and more.

Pro Tier

Unlimited AI requests, priority processing, and premium features.

How do I delete my account?

Go to the Profile tab and tap "Delete my account". This permanently removes all your data. See our detailed guide for more information.

Is my data safe?

Yes. Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We use row-level security so only you can access your data. We never sell your personal information. Read our Privacy Policy for full details.
